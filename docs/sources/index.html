Sources and further reading

The references below identify the sources used in the chapters. Links beside particular claims remain the best starting point. Repository links preserve the inspected revisions where available. Living pages were accessed on 2 October 2026. Practitioner accounts, research studies, specifications, and project records serve different purposes; their limits are discussed where they are used. No practitioner is claimed to endorse this entire approach. Interview sources refer to inspected published text or transcript sections; no complete source videos or commercial books are represented as having been consumed for this research.
